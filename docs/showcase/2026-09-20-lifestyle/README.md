App icons expanded from 6 to 12 after the additional request. The six added icons use full-bleed colored backgrounds.

24 generation calls through Codex’s native image tool, with no separately configured paid Provider. Every returned original is 1254 × 1254; the tool does not expose a model identifier. Originals are copied without recoloring, cropping or background replacement. Only board.png is a browser rendering of the HTML gallery.

The two OFFCUT images are independent new directions, not the former Hermes c1→e1→e2 refinement chain. The [historical Hermes example](../../hermes-demo/README.md) and [earlier white collection](../2026-09-white/README.md) remain unchanged.

The user’s rejection of SEAM Candidate2/5 is recorded as [negative feedback](../../research/icon-craft-2026/user-feedback-2026-09-20.json). Screenshots remain in an ignored private cache and were not sent as positive conditioning. No user selection of these 24 new images has been collected.
