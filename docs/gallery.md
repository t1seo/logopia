### Relay · quiet handoff

**v1 original → v2 refinement**

[v1 saved prompt](gallery-workflow/prompts/teum-v1.txt)

### Sprig · gentle plant care

**v1 original → v2 refinement**

<p>
<a href="gallery-workflow/images/sprig-v1.png"><img src="gallery-workflow/images/sprig-v1.png" width="208" alt="Sprig · gentle plant care · v1"></a>

**v1 original**
